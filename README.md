# Employee Workforce Intelligence System

### HR Analytics | Workforce Intelligence | Dashboard

A portfolio-based HR analytics project designed to transform structured employee data into meaningful workforce insights through headcount analysis, payroll analytics, tenure analysis, departmental workforce analysis, and attrition monitoring.

> **Personal Portfolio Project | Synthetic Workforce Data**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Employee Workforce Intelligence System demonstrates how HR teams can organize, analyze, and visualize workforce data to support data-driven HR decision-making.

The project covers the complete workflow from structured employee data to HR calculations, departmental analysis, dashboard visualization, and management-oriented workforce insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

HR teams need a clear and structured view of their workforce to monitor:

- Overall workforce size
- Active and separated employees
- Department-wise staffing
- Payroll and salary distribution
- Employee tenure
- Workforce attrition
- Department-level workforce trends

Without structured workforce analytics, identifying staffing patterns and retention concerns can become difficult.

This project addresses that need by converting employee-level data into a centralized workforce intelligence dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses **50 synthetic employee records** created specifically for portfolio and learning purposes.

Each employee record contains:

- Employee ID
- Employee Name
- Gender
- Date of Birth
- Department
- Designation
- Joining Date
- Employment Type
- Employment Status
- Location
- Manager
- Monthly Salary
- Work Shift
- Tenure

> The dataset does not represent real employees or confidential organizational information.

## 🔎 Key Insights

### Workforce Size
The analyzed workforce consists of **50 employees**, with **44 currently active**.

### Overall Attrition
There are **5 resigned employees**, resulting in an overall attrition rate of **10%**.

### Department-Level Attrition
**IT** records the highest department-level attrition rate at **75%**, followed by **Sales & Marketing at 20%**.

### Other Workforce Signals
**Kitchen** has recorded 1 resignation, while **HR** currently has 1 employee on leave.

These insights demonstrate how department-level workforce analytics can help HR identify areas requiring closer monitoring and further investigation.
